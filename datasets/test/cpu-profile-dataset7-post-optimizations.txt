Interpretation:
- The Date-based timestamp refinement that used to cost 21.2% of sampled CPU
  is gone; the whole timestamp check now fits inside isValidTimestamp at 1.9%
  because repeated values hit the value cache before the regex.
- The Zod row runner (6.0%) and Zod parsing (4.9%) are gone; row validation
  costs about 10% spread across parseDatasetRow and the per-field helpers,
  most of it in the coordinate and integer regex checks.
- The haversine formula no longer appears as a hotspot: the chord gate skips
  it for candidate pairs beyond 20.0011 m and the reported encounter
  distances still come from the exact haversine output.
- detectEncountersInGroup self time (39.8%) covers the generator frame with
  the pair loop, the chord gate and candidate bookkeeping inlined by V8.
  Detection at 240 records per group is now roughly half the process CPU.
- The async iterator chain (runMicrotasks at 4.5% plus the generator frames)
  and the line-to-string conversion (utf8Slice at 3.2%) are the next
  bounded-cost items; parseDelimitedRecord at 8.7% remains the largest
  parser cost.
- The same bounded-benefit reasoning for a detection-only worker pool from
  the previous profile still holds; the biggest remaining lever is
  pipelining or parallelizing parse plus detection across worker pools.

The profiler output was kept in the system temporary directory; no profile
binaries or generated encounter output were added to the repository.
