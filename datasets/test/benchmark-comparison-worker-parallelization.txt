Spacecom Encounter Analyzer - worker parallelization benchmark

Baseline: benchmark-results-cpu-optimizations.txt
(34.84 s dataset6, 52.41 s dataset7; encounter counts untouched from
benchmark-results-pre-optimizations.txt onwards)

Run: node apps/cli/dist/index.mjs benchmark (three consecutive full-suite
runs, raw output saved as benchmark-results-worker-parallelization.txt)

The current run keeps the full CPU-optimized serial pipeline and splits large
files across worker_threads with analyzeDatasetParallel: line-aligned chunks
planned by windowed boundary scans, one worker per queue of chunks, in-flight
timestamp groups relayed between adjacent chunks through MessagePorts so the
encounter order, counts, and error messages stay identical to the serial
path. Files smaller than two 8 MiB chunks keep the in-process streaming path.

Encounter counts and per-type splits match every previous baseline for every
dataset (4, 6, 10, 15, 21, 27, 35) in all three runs, and the pair/type
classification numbers stayed 12/9/6 and 15/12/8 for datasets 6 and 7.
Standalone CLI runs processed datasets 1 to 5 against datasets/outputs and
produced semantically identical JSON (same pairs, order, distances); datasets
6 and 7 produced JSON identical (deep equality) to a freshly computed serial
reference. All parity checks were re-run after the final refactor.

Datasets 6 and 7 together improved from 87.25 s to ~37.1 s (about 2.35x
throughput) in this capture. The machine ran with a throttled CPU/battery
profile during the capture: standalone process runs of dataset7 measured
10.12 s and 12.43 s earlier in the session (about 4.3x to 5.2x serial) with
identical output. The captured numbers above are the reproducible
conservative result.
