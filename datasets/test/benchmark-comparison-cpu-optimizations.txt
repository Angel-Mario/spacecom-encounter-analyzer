Spacecom Encounter Analyzer - CPU optimization benchmark

Previous baseline: benchmark-results-pre-optimizations.txt
Spatial index run: benchmark-results-spatial-index.txt
Current run: benchmark-results-cpu-optimizations.txt
Current run command: node apps/cli/dist/index.mjs benchmark

The current run keeps the spatial index and adds: value-cached timestamp
validation, direct positional row validation without the per-row Zod object,
an ECEF chord gate that skips the haversine formula for distant candidate
pairs, a per-group sync generator for detection, and a manual line reader
that replaces the readline interface.

Encounter counts and per-type splits match the baseline for every dataset
(4, 6, 10, 15, 21, 27, 35). Datasets 1 to 5 also produce the same JSON
encounters (same pairs, order, distances) as the previous version.

Datasets 6 and 7 together improved from 190.97 s to 87.25 s: 54.3% less
processing time against the spatial index version (about 2.19x throughput)
and 70.6% less against the original baseline (about 3.40x). Dataset 7 alone
improved from 115.50 s to 52.41 s (about 2.21x against the spatial index run)
and dataset 6 from 75.47 s to 34.84 s (about 2.17x).

Repeatability: two additional full runs measured datasets 6 and 7 together at
90.79 s and 91.40 s, so single-run variance is around 3%. Heap usage stays
near 7-38 MB; RSS stays under 200 MB per dataset, and dataset 6 no longer
shows the heap growth seen in the previous spatial-index run.

The remaining per-RSS difference between datasets (51-200 MB) tracks the
Node.js runtime baseline plus streamed group buffers, and is unchanged in
shape from the previous version.
